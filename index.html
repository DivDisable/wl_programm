3-Month Weightlifting Program

Designed to increase snatch, clean & jerk, and squat performance.

Program Overview

Goals: Increase snatch (70 → 85+ kg), clean & jerk (85 → 100+ kg), back squat (120 → 140+ kg), front squat (105 → 120+ kg).

Frequency: 5 days/week, 90–120 mins/session.

Focus: Strength, technique, and injury prevention.

Weekly Breakdown

Phase Weeks Focus
Technique & Hypertrophy 1–4 Build muscle, improve mobility, refine technique.
Strength 5–8 Increase absolute strength and power.
Peaking 9–12 Maximize 1PM and test new limits.

Daily Workouts

Day 1: Snatch + Back Squat

  • Snatch Complex: 4x3 @ 70–75%.
  • Back Squat: 5x5 @ 75–80%.
  • Accessories: Snatch-grip RDL, Plank.

Day 2: Clean & Jerk + Pulling

  • Clean & Jerk Complex: 4x3 @ 75–80%.
  • Deadlift: 4x5 @ 70–75%.
  • Accessories: Bent-over rows, Side planks.

Day 3: Front Squat + Core/Mobility

  • Front Squat: 5x5 @ 75–80%.
  • Core Circuit: Hanging leg raises, Pallof press.
  • Mobility: Ankle drills, Thoracic rotations.

Day 4: Power Lifts + Hypertrophy

  • Power Snatch: 4x3 @ 60–65%.
  • Power Clean & Jerk: 4x3 @ 65–70%.
  • RDL: 4x8 @ 60–70%.

Day 5: Heavy Lifts + Overhead Work

  • Heavy Singles: Snatch, Clean & Jerk.
  • Overhead Squat: 3x3 @ 50–60%.
  • Strict Press: 4x5 @ 70–75%.
