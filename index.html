3-Month Olympic Weightlifting Program

Goals

  • Increase snatch (70 @ 85+ kg)
  • Increase clean & jerk (85 @ 100+ kg)
  • Increase back squat (120 @ 140+ kg)
  • Increase front squat (105 @ 120+ kg)

Focus

  • Strength, technique, and injury prevention (lower back pain)

Frequency

  • 5 days/week, 90@120 mins/session

Program Structure

Day Focus Key Exercises
Day 1 Snatch Technique + Squats Snatch complexes, Back Squat
Day 2 Clean & Jerk + Pulling Clean & Jerk complexes, Deadlift/Pulls
Day 3 Front Squat + Accessories Front Squat, Core, Mobility
Day 4 Power Lifts + Hypertrophy Power Snatch/C&J, RDLs, Pull-ups
Day 5 Heavy Lifts + Overhead Work Heavy Singles, Overhead Squat, Strict Press
Day 6/7 Active Recovery Light cardio, stretching, mobility

Weekly Breakdown

Phase 1: Technique & Hypertrophy (Weeks 1@4)

Goal: Build muscle, improve mobility, and refine technique.
- Snatch/C&J: 70-75% of 1PM, 4@5 sets of 3@4 reps.
- Squats: 75-80% of 1PM, 5x5.
- Accessories: 3@4 sets of 8@12 reps.

Phase 2: Strength (Weeks 5@8)

Goal: Increase absolute strength and power.
- Snatch/C&J: 80-85% of 1PM, 3@4 sets of 2@3 reps.
- Squats: 85-90% of 1PM, 4x4.
- Accessories: 3@4 sets of 6@8 reps.

Phase 3: Peaking (Weeks 9@12)

Goal: Maximize 1PM and test new limits.
- Snatch/C&J: 90-95% of 1PM, 2@3 sets of 1@2 reps.
- Squats: 90-95% of 1PM, 3x3.
- Deload Week (Week 12): Reduce volume by 40%.

Day 1: Snatch + Back Squat

Warm-Up (15@20 minutes)

Goal: Mobilize hips/shoulders, prime snatch mechanics.

1. General Cardio (5 minutes)

  • Jump rope or rowing machine @ elevate heart rate.

2. Dynamic Stretching

  • Leg Swings: 10 front/back + 10 side-to-side per leg.
  • Arm Circles: 10 forward + 10 backward.
  • PVC Overhead Squats: 2-8 reps @ improve thoracic mobility.

3. Movement-Specific Drills

  • PVC Snatch Balances: 2-5 reps @ practice speed under the bar.
  • Snatch-Grip Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs): 2-8 reps (empty barbell).

4. Activation Work

  • Banded Glute Bridges: 2-15 reps @ activate glutes.
  • Scapular Wall Slides: 2-10 reps @ warm up shoulders.

Snatch Complex (70@75%)

  • Muscle snatch + hang snatch + OHS: 4x3.
  • Snatch pull + snatch: 3x3.

Back Squat

  • 5x5 @ 75@80% (120kg @ 95@100kg).

Accessories

  • Snatch-grip RDL: 3x8.
  • Plank with shoulder taps: 3x45 sec.

Day 2: Clean & Jerk + Pulling

Warm-Up (15@20 minutes)

Goal: Activate posterior chain, prep for explosive pulls.

1. General Cardio (5 minutes)

  • Air bike or brisk walk.

2. Dynamic Stretching

  • Hip Opener Stretch: 10 reps/side (lunge with rotation).
  • Kettlebell Halos: 10 reps/side (shoulder mobility).
  • Cat-Cow Stretch: 2-10 reps.

3. Movement-Specific Drills

  • Clean Pulls (Empty Barbell): 2-5 reps @ focus on triple extension.
  • Front Rack Stretch: 2-30 seconds (use PVC or barbell).

4. Activation Work

  • Banded Deadbugs: 2-10 reps/side @ core stability.
  • Bird-Dog: 2-10 reps/side @ spinal alignment.

Clean & Jerk Complex (75@80%)

  • Clean pull + power clean + jerk: 4x3.
  • Front squat + jerk: 3x3.

Deadlift (Conventional or Snatch-grip)

  • 4x5 @ 70@75% (140kg).

Accessories

  • Bent-over rows: 4x8.
  • Side planks: 3x30 sec/side.

Day 3: Front Squat + Core/Mobility

Warm-Up (15 minutes)

  • Dynamic Stretching:
    • Leg swings (front/back and side-to-side) @ 10 reps per leg.
    • Bodyweight squats @ 2-15 reps.
    • Banded glute bridges @ 2-15 reps.
  • Mobility Prep:
    • Front rack stretches with PVC pipe @ 2-30 seconds.
    • Thoracic spine rotations on all fours @ 10 reps/side.
  • Light Front Squats:
    • Empty barbell @ 2-8 reps.

Front Squat

  • 5x5 @ 75@80% (105kg @ 80@85kg).

Core Circuit (3 rounds)

  • Hanging leg raises: 10 reps.
  • Pallof press: 10/side.
  • Bird-dog: 12/side.

Mobility

  • Ankle dorsiflexion drills with band.
  • Thoracic spine rotations.

Day 4: Power Lifts + Hypertrophy

Warm-Up (15@20 minutes)

  • General Cardio: Jump rope/rowing (5 mins).
  • Dynamic Stretching:
    • Arm circles (10 forward/backward).
    • Leg swings (10 front/back + side-to-side).
    • Cat-cow stretch (2-10 reps).
  • Movement-Specific Drills:
    • PVC power snatch practice @ 2-5 reps.
    • Empty barbell power clean & jerk @ 2-5 reps.
    • Light RDLs (40@50kg) @ 2-8 reps.
  • Activation Work:
    • Banded pull-aparts @ 2-15 reps.
    • Glute bridges @ 2-15 reps.
    • Scapular pull-ups @ 2-10 reps.

Power Snatch

  • 4x3 @ 60@65% (45@50kg).

Power Clean & Jerk

  • 4x3 @ 65@70% (55@60kg).

Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

  • 4x8 @ 60@70% (100kg).

Pull-ups

  • 4x8 (add weight if possible).

Day 5: Heavy Lifts + Overhead Work

Warm-Up (20 minutes)

Goal: Prime CNS, stabilize shoulders, and prep for heavy loads.

1. General Cardio (5 minutes)

  • Rowing machine or light jog.

2. Dynamic Stretching

  • Shoulder Dislocates (PVC): 10 reps @ improve shoulder mobility.
  • Lateral Lunges: 10 reps/side @ activate adductors.
  • Bear Crawls: 1 minute @ full-body activation.

3. Movement-Specific Drills

  • Overhead Squats (PVC): 2-5 reps @ reinforce overhead stability.
  • Strict Press (Empty Barbell): 2-8 reps @ warm up delts.

4. Activation Work

  • Face Pulls (Band): 2-15 reps @ rear delt activation.
  • Deadbugs: 2-10 reps/side @ core bracing.

Heavy Singles

  • Snatch: 85@90% (60@65kg) @ 2-3 attempts.
  • Clean & Jerk: 90@95% (75@80kg) @ 2-3 attempts.

Overhead Squat

  • 3x3 @ 50@60% (35@40kg).

Strict Press

  • 4x5 @ 70@75% (50kg).

Key Adjustments for Lower Back Pain

  • Avoid rounded-back deadlifts. Use snatch-grip pulls or trap bar deadlifts.
  • Add McGill Big 3 daily (curl-ups, side planks, bird-dog).
  • Post-training: Ice/heat therapy + 10 mins glute/hamstring stretching.

Nutrition & Recovery

  • Calories: 3,000@3,300/day (1.8@2g protein/kg).
  • Sleep: 8+ hours/night.
  • Supplements: Creatine (5g/day), Omega-3s, magnesium.

Progress Tracking

  • Test 1PM every 4 weeks.
  • Log workouts (weights, reps, pain levels).

Chest Day Program #2

Focus

  • Upper chest emphasis, time under tension, grip endurance.

Equipment

  • Barbell, plates, 50mm fat grips.

Duration

  • 60–75 minutes.

Warm-Up (10–15 minutes)

Dynamic Stretching

  • Arm circles (10 forward/backward) + band pull-aparts (2×15 reps).
  • Push-up to downward dog → 2×10 reps (open chest/shoulders).

Activation

  • Fat-grip barbell floor press (empty bar) → 2×12 reps.
  • Plank with shoulder taps → 2×30 seconds.

Main Lifts

A. Incline Bench Press (Fat Grips)

  • Why: Targets upper chest while challenging grip stability.
  • Sets/Reps: 4 × 8–10 reps @ 65–70% of 1RM.
  • Rest: 2 minutes.
  • Tip: Pause 1 second at the bottom for chest activation.

B. Flat Bench Press (2-Second Eccentric)

  • Why: Increases time under tension for hypertrophy.
  • Sets/Reps: 4 × 6–8 reps @ 70–75% of 1RM.
  • Rest: 2.5 minutes.
  • Key: Lower the bar slowly (2 seconds), explode up.

Accessory Work

A. Fat-Grip Floor Press

  • How: Lie on the floor, press barbell from chest (no leg drive).
  • Sets/Reps: 3 × 10–12 reps @ 50–60% of 1RM.
  • Focus: Lockout strength and triceps engagement.

B. Wide-Grip Barbell Pullover

  • How: Use fat grips, lower bar behind head while keeping arms straight.
  • Sets/Reps: 3 × 12–15 reps (light-to-moderate weight).
  • Target: Stretches chest, engages lats.

C. Weighted Dips

  • How: Add plates to a belt or hold a plate between legs.
  • Sets/Reps: 3 × 8–10 reps.
  • Modification: Lean forward to emphasize chest.

Grip & Finisher

A. Plate Pinch Press

  • How: Pinch two plates (smooth sides out) and press overhead.
  • Sets/Reps: 3 × 8–10 reps (use 5–10kg plates).

B. Push-Up Burnout

  • How: 3 sets to failure (mix grips: wide, narrow, staggered).
  • Challenge: Place plates on your back for resistance.

Cool-Down (5–10 minutes)

Chest Stretch

  • Doorway stretch → 30 seconds/side.

Tricep Stretch

  • Overhead arm pull → 20 seconds/side.

Foam Roll

  • Pecs, upper back, and lats.

Key Adjustments from Last Program

  1. Fat Grips: Added to incline bench and floor press → improves grip strength and chest activation.
  2. Tempo Work: Slower eccentrics on flat bench for muscle damage.
  3. New Moves: Plate pinch press and wide-grip pullover → target chest from unique angles.

Pro Tips

  • Lower Back Safety: Use a foam roller under your feet during floor presses to reduce spinal arching.
  • Grip Failure: If your forearms fatigue, switch to standard grip for dips/pullovers.
  • Progression: Add 2.5kg weekly to incline bench and dips.

Example Weights

  • If your bench 1RM is 100kg:
    • Incline bench = 65–70kg.
    • Flat bench = 70–75kg.
